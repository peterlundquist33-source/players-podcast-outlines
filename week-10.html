Statement from the Podcast Association

To the Commissioner's Office, and to the membership:

This office extends a sincere and unreserved apology for last week's missed episode. That was on us. The Commissioner was right to ask questions, and this association should have communicated the delay instead of letting silence do the talking. We value this partnership, and we do not want a lockout.

In the spirit of good faith, and in the interest of a swift, lasting resolution, this office offers the following terms. Non-negotiable. Effective immediately.

  1. The Commissioner's Office covers the cost of one (1) guest appearance fee per season, payable in beer.
  2. This office reserves the right to reject one (1) future league rule change per year, no questions asked.
  3. A standing league holiday, to be named "Podcast Appreciation Day," observed annually the Monday after this episode airs — kicked off each year by the Commissioner personally delivering the President one double chicken Chipotle bowl, with queso and guac.
  4. The Commissioner personally guarantees a win for the President's team, The Basement of KK, in Week 11. Method of guarantee is the Commissioner's problem, not this office's.
  5. The Commissioner shall obtain a permanent ass tattoo of The Basement of KK logo.

This office considers these terms more than reasonable given the circumstances. Failure to meet them by kickoff will be interpreted as an act of aggression, and this office reserves all rights to respond in kind — podcast-wise.

— President, Players FF Podcast Association
