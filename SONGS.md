and `build.py` reads it from there — this file is the shortlist Peter picks
from, not the source of truth.

## Earmarked — hold for the right episode

These only land in a specific situation. Don't spend them on a quiet week.

- **Don't Stop Believin' — Journey** — a bottom-feeder makes an improbable
  playoff run down the stretch.
- **Separate Ways — Journey** — same arena-rock register as the Bon Jovi;
  "someday love will find you" reads as consolation for a team that keeps
  losing close ones.
- **Heavy Action** (Monday Night Football theme) — a week decided entirely on
  Monday night.
- **Are You Ready for Some Football — Hank Williams Jr.** — pure gimmick, best
  used once and never again.

## What makes a good one here

An a cappella or cold open beats an instrumental build — there's nothing to
fade under. Recognisable inside two seconds. And a song whose lyric can be
pointed at an actual result that week outperforms a generic hype track.
